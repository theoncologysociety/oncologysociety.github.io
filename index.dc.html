Who we are

A student-led society for the next generation of oncology.

We exist because the path into oncology — research, clinical care, advocacy — rarely starts in a classroom. It starts with a mentor, a lab, a conversation. We connect students to all three.

Across chapters and campuses, our members run educational series, translate research for public audiences, and build partnerships between students, clinicians and researchers.
